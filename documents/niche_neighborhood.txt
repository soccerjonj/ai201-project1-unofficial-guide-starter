Lincoln Park is a neighborhood in Chicago, Illinois with a population of 45,589. Lincoln Park is in Cook County and is one of the best places to live in Illinois. Living in Lincoln Park offers residents a dense urban feel and most residents rent their homes. In Lincoln Park there are a lot of bars, restaurants, coffee shops, and parks. Many young professionals live in Lincoln Park and residents tend to be liberal. The public schools in Lincoln Park are above average.
